# 2026 Austrian Grand Prix

Russell won at the Red Bull Ring, but the more revealing story was Max
Verstappen's recovery to second place. Verstappen lost control of the
RB22 late in qualifying and was thrown into the barriers, an accident
that limited him to fifth on the grid rather than the front row his
earlier pace suggested was possible. Starting from there, he drove
through the field to finish second, ahead of both Antonelli and Piastri.

The result was a useful data point in a season-long argument about what,
exactly, was wrong with Red Bull's car: Austria suggested the RB22 had
real one-lap and race pace when Verstappen could keep it on the road,
and that the team's problems were less about raw performance than about
reliability and the car's tendency to bite back at the limit — the same
instability that had put him in the wall in qualifying. It was a
pattern that would repeat, in a more costly form, at the very next
round in Britain.

For Mercedes, Russell's win kept the team's constructors' advantage
comfortably intact at the season's midpoint, even as Antonelli's third
place meant the two teammates were now taking turns leading races rather
than one dominating the other.
