# 2026 Japanese Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli made it two wins from three races, converting pole into a lights-to-flag victory at Suzuka ahead of Piastri and Leclerc, with teammate Russell only fourth. Coming a week after his breakthrough win in China, Suzuka made clear that result wasn't a one-off: back-to-back victories at two very different circuits pointed to a Mercedes package that was fast everywhere, not just suited to one kind of track.

Red Bull's early-season struggles continued. Max Verstappen qualified eleventh and could only recover to eighth, still well off the pace of the front-runners even on a weekend without the kind of mechanical failure that had ended his Chinese Grand Prix early. A single safety car period, triggered by retirements for Lance Stroll and Oliver Bearman, did little to change the competitive order at the front.

Three rounds into the new regulations, the season's shape was hardening: Mercedes winning consistently through Antonelli, Ferrari showing genuine pace without yet finding victory, and Red Bull still searching for answers with the RB22.
