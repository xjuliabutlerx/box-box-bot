# 2026 Barcelona Grand Prix

Lewis Hamilton won in Barcelona, passing polesitter George Russell early and holding on for Ferrari's first victory of the season — and, just as significantly, the race that finally ended Kimi Antonelli's five-race winning streak. Antonelli didn't simply lose a fight for the win, either: he retired from third on the grid with two-thirds of the race complete, meaning the streak broke not on pace but on reliability.

Lando Norris was third for McLaren, ahead of a recovering Max Verstappen in fourth from fifth on the grid — a solid if unspectacular result that continued Red Bull's pattern of showing real pace without yet finding a way to the top step. Charles Leclerc, starting back in tenth after a poor qualifying, retired from the second Ferrari alongside four other cars across two Virtual Safety Car periods.

For Ferrari, Hamilton's win was a long-awaited return to victory lane in red, and a needed answer to the question of whether the team could actually convert its early-season pace into results rather than just podiums.
