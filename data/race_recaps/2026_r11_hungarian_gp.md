# 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix

Lando Norris took his first win of the season in Budapest, converting pole position into a lights-to-flag victory ahead of a recovering Max Verstappen and Kimi Antonelli, who climbed from seventh on the grid to the final podium spot. For McLaren, it was the breakthrough result the team had been chasing all year — the first sign that the reigning champions had found genuine pace with the new regulations rather than just incremental progress.

Verstappen's second place, matching his best result of the season, added to a growing body of evidence that the RB22 had real pace when it stayed reliable and out of the walls — a "when," not "if," that had defined Red Bull's season. Oscar Piastri's difficult year continued: he retired from third on the grid with two laps to go, one of several disappointing results in what had become a rough title defense alongside Norris.

Antonelli's podium extended his championship lead further still, but Norris's win — his first of two in a row heading into the summer break — was the result that mattered most for the shape of the season ahead.
