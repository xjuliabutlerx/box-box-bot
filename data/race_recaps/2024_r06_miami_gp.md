# 2024 Miami Grand Prix

Lando Norris won his first Formula 1 race in Miami, ending Max Verstappen's dominant start to the season and delivering McLaren's first victory since 2021. Norris started fifth but jumped ahead during a late safety car period, restarting directly behind poleman Verstappen and passing him for the lead shortly after - a straightforward, clean overtake that ended a run of over a hundred races without a win for Norris personally and confirmed that McLaren's early-season pace upgrade had turned it into a genuine race-winning car, not just a podium contender.

The result carried real weight beyond a single race. Verstappen and Red Bull had won every round to that point in the season, and Miami was the first time in 2024 that another team had looked capable of beating them on merit rather than through misfortune or a Red Bull off-day. For Norris specifically, it ended years of near-misses and second-place finishes that had built a narrative around him as one of the best drivers yet to win a race.

Charles Leclerc completed the podium in third for Ferrari, but the day belonged to Norris and McLaren. The win proved to be the first sign of a genuine title-contending package that would keep Norris in championship contention deep into the season and set up a fierce constructors' fight between McLaren and Ferrari that would run all the way to the final round in Abu Dhabi.
