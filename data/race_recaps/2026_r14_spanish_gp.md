# 2026 Spanish Grand Prix

Formula 1 raced in Madrid for the first time, and Kimi Antonelli made himself the first winner at the new circuit, converting second on the grid into victory ahead of Max Verstappen and polesitter Lando Norris. It was Antonelli's eighth win of a season that had put the championship almost entirely out of reach for the rest of the field.

The more interesting fight was for second. Verstappen jumped Norris with a well-timed undercut, pitting for hard tires a lap before his rival — and the gap was sealed when Norris's own stop the following lap ran long at nearly thirty-five seconds, one of the slowest of the afternoon. With no second stop needed for either driver and a clean pit lane execution from Verstappen, the position swap held to the finish. Charles Leclerc was fourth, with Ferrari's other car retiring early: Lewis Hamilton was out by lap six, one of four retirements on the day.

A single Virtual Safety Car period was the only interruption on an otherwise straightforward race weekend for Madrid's Formula 1 debut.
