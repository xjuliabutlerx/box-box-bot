# 2016 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

Nico Rosberg won the world championship by finishing second behind teammate Lewis Hamilton at the season finale in Abu Dhabi, closing out a Mercedes rivalry that had run through the entire year. Hamilton, needing to win with Rosberg finishing no higher than third to keep his own title hopes alive, controlled the race from pole but backed the pack up for most of the second half in a deliberate attempt to bring Rosberg into range of the pursuing Ferraris and force him into a mistake or a lost position.

The tactic never quite worked. Rosberg drove a disciplined race under enormous pressure, holding off both Sebastian Vettel and Max Verstappen despite lap times far slower than Hamilton could otherwise have set, and crossed the line second - enough to win the title by five points. Mercedes' pit wall repeatedly radioed Hamilton to speed up during the closing laps; he ignored the instructions, prioritizing his own win over any team consideration, in a public display of just how far the teammates' relationship had deteriorated over the season.

It was Rosberg's only world championship, capping a rivalry with Hamilton that had included multiple on-track collisions across the year, most notably the Spanish Grand Prix crash that took both cars out on the opening lap. Five days after winning the title, Rosberg announced his retirement from Formula 1 - a stunning end to the story that few had seen coming even as he stood on the podium in Abu Dhabi.
