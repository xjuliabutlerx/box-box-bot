# 2025 Singapore Grand Prix

Russell took the win under the Marina Bay lights, with Verstappen
second, but the story for McLaren's title fight was Norris making
contact with teammate Piastri, who fell to fourth behind his teammate's
third place. Unlike the Monza pit-stop swap two rounds earlier, this
contact drew no team order and no visible in-race consequence for
Norris — a contrast that did not go unnoticed given how the papaya rules
had just been invoked in the opposite direction at Monza.

Taken together, Monza and Singapore became the two reference points for
criticism of how McLaren managed its drivers in 2025: a team order that
cost Piastri track position in Italy, followed by contact that cost him
more points in Singapore with no equivalent intervention. Whether or not
the team's decisions were actually inconsistent, the pattern was enough
to make "papaya rules" a recurring flashpoint in coverage of the
championship battle for the rest of the season, and a topic both drivers
were still being asked about heading into 2026.

Championship-wise, Singapore tightened the gap between the two
teammates further, part of the broader mid-to-late-season trend of
Piastri's advantage eroding while Norris and Verstappen both closed in
from behind.
