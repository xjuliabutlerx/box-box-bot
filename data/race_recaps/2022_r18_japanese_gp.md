# 2022 Japanese Grand Prix

Max Verstappen won a rain-hit Japanese Grand Prix at Suzuka to clinch his second consecutive world championship, though the manner of the confirmation caused immediate confusion in the moments after the checkered flag. The race started behind the safety car in heavy rain and was later red-flagged after Carlos Sainz crashed early on, resuming only after a lengthy delay; because so few green-flag racing laps were completed, most in the paddock initially assumed only half points would be awarded under the sport's reduced-points rules for a shortened race.

Verstappen himself was informed on the podium that he had not yet won the title, only for officials to clarify shortly afterward that a change to the points regulations, quietly introduced before the season, meant full points were actually awarded provided a minimum number of laps had been completed under green-flag conditions - which this race had. The title was confirmed retroactively, well after the podium celebrations had already begun under the mistaken belief that the championship remained undecided.

The confusion overshadowed what was otherwise a dominant, largely processional win from pole in atrocious conditions, and prompted the FIA to clarify and simplify its points-scoring regulations for shortened races going forward. Verstappen's second title came with four races still to spare, extending a dominant campaign that would finish with a record-breaking points total for the year.
