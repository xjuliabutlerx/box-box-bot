# 2021 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

Max Verstappen won the world championship on the final lap of the final race of the season, passing Lewis Hamilton for the lead moments after a late safety car restart that remains one of the most disputed decisions in the sport's history. Hamilton had controlled the race for most of the distance and looked set to win both the race and an eighth world title, until a late-race crash for Nicholas Latifi brought out a safety car that the two title rivals' teams interpreted completely differently.

Race director Michael Masi allowed only the lapped cars directly between Verstappen and Hamilton to unlap themselves before restarting the race for one final lap, rather than either all lapped cars or none, in a decision that departed from the sport's own standard procedure and left Verstappen on fresh soft tires directly behind Hamilton, who was on worn hard tires, for a single-lap shootout to the flag. Verstappen passed him at Turn 5 and held on to win both the race and the championship by eight points, denying Hamilton what would have been a record eighth title.

Mercedes protested the result immediately and pursued it further in the following days, but the outcome stood. The controversy prompted a significant review of race-direction procedures for the following season, including the introduction of a rotating race director role, and the finale remains widely regarded as one of the most contentious conclusions to a championship in Formula 1 history.
