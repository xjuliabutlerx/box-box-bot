# 2025 Italian Grand Prix

Max Verstappen won at Monza, but the race that mattered most to the
championship storyline happened behind him, between the two McLarens.
Norris and Piastri ran second and third for most of the race; when both
pitted, Piastri's stop was flawless while Norris's was slow, dropping
him behind his teammate. Because the position swap was caused by
McLaren's own pit-lane error rather than being earned on track, the team
ordered Piastri to hand second place back to Norris before the finish.

The call reopened a running argument about McLaren's so-called "papaya
rules" — an informal understanding, dating back to 2024, that the two
drivers should race hard but not at each other's expense or the team's.
In practice, applying that principle consistently proved difficult:
Monza was widely read as the moment the rule benefited Norris at
Piastri's expense, and it fed a narrative — fair or not — that papaya
rules only got invoked when they favored the British driver. That
perception would resurface just two rounds later in Singapore, when
Norris made contact with Piastri without consequence.

For a season McLaren ultimately won both championships in, Monza is the
race most associated with the team's internal tension: proof that
running two title-contending drivers in the same car is as much a
management problem as an engineering one.
