# 2025 Qatar Grand Prix

Verstappen delivered a dominant win in Qatar, with Piastri second, Carlos
Sainz a surprise third for Williams, and Norris only fourth. It was the
result that turned Verstappen's late-season form from "impressive" into
"genuinely threatening": going into the second-to-last round of the
year, he arrived just twelve points behind championship leader Norris,
having been as far back as 104 points at Piastri's peak earlier in the
season.

That swing — well over a hundred points clawed back in a matter of
months — was one of the largest late-season comebacks in recent
championship history, and it is why Qatar is remembered as the race
that set up a genuine three-way finale in Abu Dhabi rather than a
coronation for whichever McLaren driver arrived in better shape. Norris
still held the championship lead after Qatar, but only barely, and
Verstappen went into the final round with a real, arithmetic chance at a
fifth consecutive title — which would have equaled Michael Schumacher's
record.

For Piastri, a strong second place in Qatar kept him mathematically
alive too, but by this point in the season the championship had
effectively narrowed to a fight between his teammate and Verstappen,
with Piastri needing results to go wrong for both of them simultaneously.
