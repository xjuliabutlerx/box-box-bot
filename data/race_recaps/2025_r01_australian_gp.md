# 2025 Australian Grand Prix

Lando Norris opened the 2025 season with a win at a wet, treacherous
Albert Park, beating Max Verstappen into second with George Russell
completing the podium for Mercedes. It was a scrappy race defined by
changing conditions: several front-runners struggled for grip as the
track dried unevenly, and Norris's composure through the tricky middle
stint was the difference between the two title favorites.

The result carried extra weight beyond three points on a scoreboard. It
was Norris's first win of the year and it denied Verstappen the kind of
dominant start that had defined his previous four championship-winning
seasons. Verstappen had entered 2025 on a run of consecutive titles, and
a slower, more competitive Red Bull car meant Melbourne set the tone for
a season where he would have to fight for wins rather than assume them.

McLaren's other driver, Oscar Piastri, was not on the podium in
Melbourne, but the team's pace across both cars signaled that 2025 would
likely be a McLaren-versus-Red Bull, and possibly a McLaren-versus-McLaren,
championship fight rather than a Verstappen procession. That McLaren
intra-team dynamic — which driver gets priority when both are competitive
— would become the defining subplot of the season by the time the team
reached Monza in September.
