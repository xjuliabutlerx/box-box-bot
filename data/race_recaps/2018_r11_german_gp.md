# 2018 German Grand Prix

Lewis Hamilton won his home-continent rival's home race from nowhere, recovering from fourteenth on the grid after Sebastian Vettel crashed out of the lead at his own home Grand Prix with just a handful of laps remaining. Vettel had dominated most of the race, building a comfortable lead in changeable, drying conditions, before losing the rear of his Ferrari on a damp patch of track and sliding into the barriers - a mistake that handed the win directly to Hamilton, who had struggled in qualifying but come alive once the track began to dry.

The crash was a significant swing in a championship fight that had been close for most of the season: Vettel had led the standings for much of the first half of the year, and a win at Hockenheim in front of his home crowd would have extended that advantage further. Instead, the error - and Hamilton's opportunistic recovery drive through the field - turned the momentum decisively toward Mercedes for the second half of the season.

Vettel and Ferrari never fully recaptured the championship initiative after Germany. Hamilton went on to win his fifth world title that year, and the Hockenheim crash was widely remembered afterward as the moment the 2018 season turned, even though several rounds of racing still remained.
