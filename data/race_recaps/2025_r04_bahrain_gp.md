# 2025 Bahrain Grand Prix

Oscar Piastri won in Bahrain, with Russell second and teammate Norris
third, and in doing so took over the lead of the 2025 drivers'
championship. It was a quiet-looking result on paper — a comfortable
McLaren win, business as usual for the sport's fastest car — but it
marked the start of the longest championship-lead streak of the season:
Piastri would hold top spot in the standings for seventeen of the
season's twenty-four rounds, all the way through to the Mexican Grand
Prix in October.

What made Bahrain notable in hindsight is less the race itself than what
it kicked off. Through the first four rounds, Norris and Verstappen had
traded results at the front, but from Bahrain onward Piastri became the
most consistent points-scorer in the field, capitalizing on McLaren's
car advantage more effectively than his more error-prone teammate. That
consistency is exactly why, when Piastri's form eventually dipped in the
second half of the season, it opened the door for both Norris and a
resurgent Verstappen to close in — a swing that would ultimately decide
the championship in Abu Dhabi.

Bahrain is also a useful marker for how differently McLaren's two
drivers built their seasons: Piastri front-loaded his points advantage
early, while Norris's title-winning campaign was built more on
recovering from a mid-season deficit than on ever leading from the
front.
