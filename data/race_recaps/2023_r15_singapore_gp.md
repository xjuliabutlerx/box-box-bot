# 2023 Singapore Grand Prix

Carlos Sainz won from pole in Singapore, the only race Max Verstappen failed to win across the entire 2023 season, snapping a dominant run that had included ten consecutive victories earlier in the year. Verstappen qualified a distant eleventh after struggling with Red Bull's setup around the tight Marina Bay street circuit and could only recover to fifth in the race itself, unable to make his usual impact on a track that historically rewards qualifying pace over raw straight-line speed.

Sainz controlled the race from the front, holding off a late charge from Lando Norris and George Russell in the closing laps after a virtual safety car period bunched the field up and briefly threatened his lead. The win was Ferrari's first of the season and gave Sainz his second career victory, in what became the single result that kept Red Bull's win record from 2023 short of a perfect season.

In a year that was otherwise a near-total procession for Verstappen and Red Bull - he would finish the season with nineteen wins from twenty-two races - Singapore stood out as the one weekend where the dominant car simply didn't suit the circuit, a reminder that even the most one-sided championship campaigns in the sport's history still have their exceptions.
