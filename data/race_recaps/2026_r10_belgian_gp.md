# 2026 Belgian Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli returned to winning form at Spa-Francorchamps after Barcelona's blip, taking his sixth win of the season from pole ahead of Charles Leclerc, with Max Verstappen completing the podium in third. For Red Bull, third place carried extra significance: it came on the same rear wing the team had reverted to following the component failure that ended Verstappen's British Grand Prix, a deliberate step back to a known-reliable part while a permanent fix was developed.

The choice looked justified by more than just Verstappen's result. Isack Hadjar produced the drive of the weekend, recovering from twenty-first on the grid to sixth — suggesting the RB22's underlying pace remained genuinely competitive once the car stayed in one piece. Lando Norris, by contrast, could only manage seventh from a lowly thirteenth on the grid, a quiet weekend for McLaren. George Russell retired from third on the grid without completing a lap, his second zero-lap-type result of the season.

A safety car and two Virtual Safety Car periods punctuated the race, but didn't disturb the front order once it was established.
