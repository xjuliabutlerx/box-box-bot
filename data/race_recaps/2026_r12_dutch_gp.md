# 2026 Dutch Grand Prix

Lando Norris won at Zandvoort, with Antonelli second and Russell third,
snapping Mercedes' hold on the top step after a season in which the team
had otherwise dominated. For McLaren, still adjusting to life as a
Mercedes-powered team no longer running away with the championship the
way it had in 2025, it was the clearest sign yet that the reigning
champions could still win races outright once they found the right
setup for the new regulations.

It didn't change the shape of the championship fight, though. Antonelli
carried a commanding points lead into the summer break and extended it
further with Zandvoort's second place, while Norris's win moved him up
the standings without seriously threatening the top of the table.
Twelve rounds into the season, Mercedes' early-season advantage — built
on Antonelli's run of wins in China, Japan, and Miami, and reinforced by
consistent podiums from Russell — had proven durable rather than a
product of simply being first to understand the new rules.

For Verstappen and Red Bull, the Dutch Grand Prix carried extra weight
as Verstappen's home race, but with the team still searching for its
first win of the season, Zandvoort was another round spent chasing the
podium rather than fighting for the top step in front of his home crowd.
