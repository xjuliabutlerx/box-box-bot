# 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix

George Russell converted pole position into a win at Baku, but Max Verstappen made it uncomfortably close. A recovery drive from eighth on the grid brought Verstappen all the way back to Russell's gearbox, the two crossing the line just 0.196 seconds apart — the tightest margin of the season so far. Isack Hadjar took the final podium spot in third, while Charles Leclerc slipped from second on the grid to fourth. Kimi Antonelli salvaged fifth from sixteenth on the grid, a quiet recovery drive that kept his points lead intact without needing to fight at the front.

The race was broken up by two separate safety car periods and finished with seven retirements, making it one of the most chaotic weekends of the year. The first safety car, deployed on lap 31, followed Alexander Albon parking his Williams trackside two laps earlier. The second came almost immediately after the first restart: a three-car collision at Turn 1 eliminated Lando Norris, Pierre Gasly, and Franco Colapinto in one move, with stewards handing Colapinto a post-race ten-second penalty for causing it. Aston Martin's Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll had already retired early, on laps 20 and 7 respectively, and Valtteri Bottas became the race's seventh retirement after a Turn 15 incident with just two laps to go.

Russell also took fastest lap, set on lap 49 on soft tires in the closing stretch after the second restart — a fitting final word in a race he controlled from pole to flag despite Verstappen's late charge.
