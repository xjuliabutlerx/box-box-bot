# 2021 British Grand Prix

Lewis Hamilton won at Silverstone after a first-lap collision with championship rival Max Verstappen sent Verstappen's car into the barriers at Copse corner at high speed, in one of the most controversial flashpoints of a bitterly fought title fight. Verstappen, who had entered the race leading the championship, was taken to hospital for precautionary checks after an impact measured at over 50G, while Hamilton received a ten-second penalty for causing the collision but recovered from it during the race to win regardless.

The incident split opinion sharply across the sport. Red Bull and Verstappen's camp considered the penalty far too lenient given the violence of the crash and argued Hamilton had been the aggressor in a move that could easily have ended far worse; Mercedes and Hamilton's supporters pointed to it as a racing incident, with both drivers committed to a corner neither was fully willing to concede. The stewards' ten-second time penalty, applied without forcing an immediate pit stop, allowed Hamilton to serve it and still win the race in front of his home crowd.

The crash and its aftermath hardened what was already a tense, personal rivalry between Hamilton and Verstappen for the rest of the season, setting the tone for further flashpoints at Monza and elsewhere before the title was ultimately decided in the most controversial circumstances of all at the season finale in Abu Dhabi.
