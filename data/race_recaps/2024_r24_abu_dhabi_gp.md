# 2024 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

Lando Norris won the season finale in Abu Dhabi from pole, but the bigger prize went to his team: McLaren clinched the constructors' championship, its first since 2008, ending a sixteen-year wait that had spanned some of the team's leanest years in its history. Carlos Sainz was second for Ferrari, while Charles Leclerc recovered from a poor nineteenth on the grid to finish third and keep Ferrari's own constructors' bid alive until the final laps, though it fell just short of McLaren's tally.

The constructors' fight had run all season and came down to the wire: McLaren, Ferrari, and Red Bull were separated by a relatively narrow margin heading into the finale, with points spread across two drivers per team meaning the destination of the title depended on the combined result of both cars, not just a single result at the front. Norris's win, combined with a strong recovery from teammate Oscar Piastri, was enough to secure it for McLaren regardless of how the Ferrari drivers finished.

For a team that had spent much of the preceding decade in the midfield, the title was a significant marker of McLaren's rebuild under a new technical and management structure, and closed out a season in which Norris had also mounted a serious, if ultimately unsuccessful, challenge to Max Verstappen for the drivers' title.
