# 2017 Singapore Grand Prix

Lewis Hamilton won from fifth on the grid in Singapore after a chaotic opening lap eliminated several of his closest championship rivals, in one of the biggest title-fight swings of the 2017 season. Sebastian Vettel, who had started on pole, was collected in a multi-car incident at the first corner alongside title rival Max Verstappen and Vettel's own teammate Kimi Räikkönen - all three retired on the spot in a wet-weather pileup that instantly reshaped the race.

With his two closest title threats gone before the first lap was even complete, Hamilton inherited a lead he never seriously looked like losing, eventually winning ahead of Daniel Ricciardo and his own teammate Valtteri Bottas. The result turned what had been a tightening championship fight into a rout: Vettel's zero-points weekend, set against Hamilton's win, swung the title picture decisively in Mercedes' favor with a third of the season still to run.

Ferrari's championship challenge never fully recovered. Vettel would go on to suffer further reliability issues in the following rounds, and Hamilton wrapped up his fourth world title with two races still to spare - a campaign in which Singapore's first-lap chaos, more than any single dominant Mercedes performance, marked the turning point.
