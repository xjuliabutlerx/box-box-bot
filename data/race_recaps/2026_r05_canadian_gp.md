# 2026 Canadian Grand Prix

A chaotic race in Montreal produced Kimi Antonelli's fourth win in five rounds, extending the run of dominance that began in China, but the result masked one of the most attrition-heavy weekends of the season. Three separate Virtual Safety Car periods and six retirements — including polesitter George Russell, who was out by lap twenty-nine with a mechanical issue — turned what looked like a straightforward Mercedes weekend into a survival exercise for everyone else.

Lewis Hamilton capitalized best on the carnage, recovering from fifth on the grid to finish second, while Max Verstappen took his best result of the season to that point in third. For McLaren, the round was a write-off: Lando Norris retired from third on the grid, compounding a difficult start to the title defense that had already seen teammate Oscar Piastri fail to start the season opener.

With Russell's retirement, Antonelli's points advantage in the drivers' championship grew even larger than the race result alone suggested — his nearest teammate had come away with nothing.
