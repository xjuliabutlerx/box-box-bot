# 2026 Italian Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli produced the drive of the season at Monza, recovering from a disastrous nineteenth on the grid to win the race outright — carving through the entire field on pure pace to beat teammate George Russell, who had started from the front row. It was a result that underlined just how dominant Mercedes' package had been all year: even a poor qualifying couldn't stop their lead driver from winning.

The story at the front of the grid was just as dramatic. Pierre Gasly took a surprise pole for Alpine, only to fade to seventh in the race itself, while Ferrari's home weekend turned into a nightmare for the Tifosi: Charles Leclerc retired after completing just a single lap from third on the grid. Max Verstappen was the best of the rest in third, continuing his run of solid if title-irrelevant results, with Lando Norris fourth.

A safety car and a Virtual Safety Car period did nothing to slow Antonelli's march through the field — if anything, Monza only reinforced that his championship lead was built on genuine race-winning pace, not just a fast car in clean air.
