# 2025 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

Max Verstappen won the season finale in Abu Dhabi, with Piastri second
and Norris third — and it still wasn't enough. Norris arrived at the
last round of the year holding a slim championship lead over Verstappen,
and a podium finish, even from third place, behind both title rivals,
was exactly enough for him to be crowned champion by two points. It was
Lando Norris's first world title, McLaren's first drivers' championship
since Lewis Hamilton in 2008, and the end of Verstappen's run of four
consecutive titles.

The finale was the payoff of two separate storylines that had been
building for months. Verstappen's win completed a comeback from a
104-point deficit down to just two at the flag — arguably the closest a
driver has come to overturning a near-insurmountable gap in the modern
points era — while Norris's title was really secured months earlier by
consistently finishing races that Piastri, who led the championship for
seventeen rounds, did not. Piastri's own late collapse from championship
leader to third in the final standings, thirteen points off Norris, made
him the biggest loser of the season despite arguably having the
strongest car for most of it.

McLaren also wrapped up a second consecutive constructors' title, with
Mercedes a distant second — a result that made 2025 a triumphant year
for the team on paper, even as the papaya-rules controversies from Monza
and Singapore left lingering questions about how it managed the fight
between its own two drivers.
