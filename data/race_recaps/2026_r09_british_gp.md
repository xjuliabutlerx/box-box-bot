# 2026 British Grand Prix

Charles Leclerc won at Silverstone, with Russell second and Hamilton
third, giving Ferrari a rare win over the two dominant Mercedes drivers.
For Red Bull, though, the weekend was another step backward: Max
Verstappen, who had shown genuine race pace with a recovery drive to
second place just one round earlier in Austria, spun out of the race and
retired after a rear-wing fault, classified last of the finishers.

The failure was mechanically unrelated to the qualifying crash in
Austria, but the two incidents together hardened a narrative that had
been building since the season opener: Red Bull's issues in 2026 weren't
simply about the RB22 lacking outright pace, but about a car that was
fundamentally difficult to keep on the road and prone to component
failures under load. The team would go on to revert to a previous
rear-wing design at the following round in Belgium while it worked on a
fix, rather than press ahead with the updated part that had failed at
Silverstone.

By this point in the season, Red Bull remained the only team among the
top four in the constructors' standings without a win, a striking
reversal for a team that had won four consecutive drivers' titles with
Verstappen through 2024.
