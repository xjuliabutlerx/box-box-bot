# 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix

Sergio Pérez won his first Formula 1 race at the Sakhir Grand Prix, run on Bahrain's shorter outer layout one week after the standard Bahrain Grand Prix, in a chaotic race that also saw Mercedes come close to a historic upset of its own. With Lewis Hamilton absent after testing positive for COVID-19, Mercedes called up reserve driver George Russell - then a Williams driver with no prior points to his name - to fill in, and Russell spent most of the race dominant, leading comfortably in what looked set to be a maiden win in only his first start for the team.

Mercedes' pit crew then made a rare double error, fitting the wrong tires to both of their cars during a safety car pit stop and having to call both Russell and teammate Valtteri Bottas back in to correct the mistake, dropping Russell from the lead. A late puncture compounded the misfortune further, and Russell ultimately salvaged only ninth place - a handful of points rather than the win his pace all day had deserved, in a result that felt far crueler than the drive itself.

That left the win to Pérez, who recovered from an early spin and a lap deficit to storm back through the field for Racing Point - the first win of his career after 190 Grand Prix starts, the longest wait for a maiden victory in the sport's history at the time. It proved to be a pivotal result for his own future, helping secure the seat at Red Bull that he would take up the following season.
