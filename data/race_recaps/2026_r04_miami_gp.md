# 2026 Miami Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli won in Miami, beating Norris and Piastri's McLarens into
second and third with Russell fourth, making it his third consecutive
victory following wins in China and Japan. The result made him the
youngest driver in Formula 1 history to win three straight grands prix,
a milestone that turned a promising start to the season into a genuine
title campaign.

What made the streak notable wasn't just Antonelli's pace but its
timing: it came directly on the heels of a difficult rookie year in
which he was regularly outperformed by his more experienced Mercedes
teammate. Team principal Toto Wolff had described Antonelli as
potential world-champion material even during that shaky 2025 season,
and Miami was the result that made the claim look prescient rather than
generous. By the summer break, Antonelli would build this early
advantage into a lead of roughly fifty points over his nearest rival,
Hamilton, in the drivers' standings.

For Red Bull, Miami offered a partial recovery: Verstappen finished
fifth, his best result of the season to that point, though still a long
way from contending for wins. Four rounds in, Mercedes' early-season
form was looking less like a fluke of the new regulations and more like
a genuine competitive advantage the rest of the grid would need months
to close.
