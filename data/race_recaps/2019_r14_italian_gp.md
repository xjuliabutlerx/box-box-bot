# 2019 Italian Grand Prix

Charles Leclerc won at Monza, Ferrari's home race, for his second consecutive victory following Spa - a result made harder than it should have been by a sustained, race-long defense against Lewis Hamilton, who spent much of the second half of the race in Leclerc's mirrors looking for a way past. Leclerc controlled the pace just enough to keep Hamilton at arm's length, backing the field up through the Ascari chicane and the Parabolica in a defensive display that Ferrari's home crowd, the Tifosi, celebrated as much for its toughness as for the result itself.

The win meant even more given the setting: Monza is Ferrari's home race, and a Ferrari victory there - their first at the track since 2010 - carried weight well beyond the points it delivered. Leclerc, in his first season with the team, had gone from a promising newcomer to a back-to-back race winner in the space of two rounds, doing it in front of the most passionate fanbase in the sport.

Valtteri Bottas completed the podium in third for Mercedes, but the day belonged entirely to Leclerc and Ferrari. The two consecutive wins at Spa and Monza stood as the high point of Ferrari's 2019 campaign, a season that otherwise saw Mercedes dominate both championships comfortably.
