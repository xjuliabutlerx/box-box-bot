# Circuit of the Americas

COTA was purpose-built for Formula 1 with inspiration drawn from several classic corners around the world, most notably a steep uphill run to Turn 1 modeled loosely on Silverstone's Maggotts-Becketts, followed by a long, winding "esses" section and a long back straight. The elevation change at Turn 1 in particular creates a genuine passing opportunity right at the start of the lap.

Overtaking is generally good by modern standards - the wide track and multiple straights give drivers several realistic chances, particularly into Turn 1 and Turn 12. Tire degradation is moderate, and both one- and two-stop strategies are common depending on conditions. Texas weather can be unpredictable in autumn, occasionally bringing rain that adds a wet-strategy wildcard to what's usually a fairly conventional strategic race.
