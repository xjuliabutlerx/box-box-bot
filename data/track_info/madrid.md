# Madring (Madrid)

Madrid's circuit made its Formula 1 debut this season, built around the IFEMA exhibition center on the city's outskirts as a hybrid layout combining permanent track sections with public roads - a similar concept to Miami and Las Vegas, adding a purpose-built circuit to a street-adjacent setting rather than running entirely on existing city streets. It was announced as part of Formula 1's push into new markets alongside Las Vegas and Miami, and was designed with a long straight intended to promote overtaking, paired with a tighter technical section.

As a brand-new venue, teams arrived with far less prior data than at an established circuit, so setup choices and tire strategy were more of a guessing game than usual in the early running. Expect the "typical" strategic character of Madrid - one-stop versus two-stop tendencies, which corners actually produce overtakes in race trim - to become clearer after a season or two of real results to draw on, the same way it took time to properly understand Miami and Las Vegas after their own debuts.
