# Autódromo José Carlos Pace (Interlagos)

Interlagos is one of the most beloved circuits on the calendar - a short, anti-clockwise lap with significant elevation change, run counter-clockwise (a rarity that unusually loads the drivers' necks in the opposite direction from most circuits) through the outskirts of São Paulo. The uphill start-finish straight into the tight Senna S is one of the most famous corner sequences in the sport.

The short lap and long straights relative to lap distance make Interlagos a strong overtaking circuit, and it's produced some of Formula 1's most dramatic finishes for exactly that reason. Weather at altitude in São Paulo is notoriously changeable, with rain a real possibility even on an otherwise dry weekend, and the track's history of chaotic, rain-affected races has made it a frequent decisive round late in the championship.
