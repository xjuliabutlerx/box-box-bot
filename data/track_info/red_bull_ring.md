# Red Bull Ring

One of the shortest laps on the calendar at just over 4.3 km, set in the Styrian hills with significant elevation change and a rapid lap time despite having only a handful of corners. The layout is short and flowing, with three long straights connected by heavy-braking corners, which keeps lap times low and the field tightly bunched.

That short lap length and bunched field make Austria one of the more overtaking-friendly circuits, especially into Turns 1, 3, and 4, all strong DRS zones. Because the lap is so short, cars complete more laps than almost anywhere else on the calendar, which changes the math on pit windows and undercuts - the "cost" of pitting relative to total race distance is higher than at a longer circuit. Tire degradation is moderate, and both one- and two-stop strategies are viable depending on temperature.
