# Yas Marina Circuit

The traditional season finale, run at twilight as the sky darkens over the course of the race, set around Yas Island's marina and the Yas Viceroy hotel. A 2021 layout reconfiguration removed several of the low-speed, tightly-walled sections that had previously made overtaking notoriously difficult, replacing them with faster, wider corners and significantly improving the racing.

Multiple long straights and DRS zones now give Yas Marina genuinely good overtaking opportunities, a major change from its earlier reputation as one of the calendar's most processional circuits. Tire degradation is moderate, and both one- and two-stop strategies are viable. As a season-ending round, races here often carry unusual championship stakes, which can push teams toward more aggressive strategic gambles than they'd normally take at a mid-season event.
