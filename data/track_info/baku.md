# Baku City Circuit

Baku is a study in contrasts: one of the longest, fastest straights on the calendar runs alongside the Caspian Sea, feeding into an extremely tight, narrow section winding through Baku's old city walls barely wide enough for a Formula 1 car. Few circuits demand such different skills within a single lap - low-downforce top-speed running on the straight, then centimeter-precision car placement through the castle section moments later.

The long straight makes Baku one of the best overtaking circuits on the calendar, with slipstreams regularly producing passes into Turn 1. The narrow castle section is a common source of red flags and safety cars, since there's no room for recovery from even a small mistake, making Baku one of the more strategically chaotic races of the year - and one where an early safety car can completely reshuffle a straightforward one-stop race plan.
