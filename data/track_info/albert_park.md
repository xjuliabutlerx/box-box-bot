# Albert Park Circuit (Melbourne)

Albert Park is a 5.28 km permanent circuit built on public roads around a lake in central Melbourne, traditionally the season opener. A series of resurfacing and layout tweaks in the early 2020s smoothed out what was once a notoriously bumpy track and opened up several genuine overtaking zones, so it now races more like a proper permanent circuit than the street-track reputation it once had.

Overtaking is moderate: the long start-finish straight into Turn 1 and the back straight into Turn 9 are the two main passing zones, both aided by DRS. Tire degradation is generally low-to-moderate, and a one-stop strategy is typical barring a safety car - which is a real possibility given the walls that still line much of the lap. As a season opener, it also tends to expose which teams have actually understood a new set of regulations, since there's no prior-race form to go on.
