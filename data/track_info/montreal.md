# Circuit Gilles Villeneuve

A low-downforce, stop-start circuit built on an island in the St. Lawrence River, defined by long straights connected by tight chicanes and a heavy reliance on the brakes. The final chicane leads straight into a concrete wall close enough that it's been nicknamed the "Wall of Champions" for catching out several title winners over the years - shorthand for how unforgiving the track is despite looking simple on paper.

Overtaking is generally good: multiple straights and heavy braking zones make for several realistic DRS passing opportunities. Brake wear is a genuine concern given how much of the lap is spent slowing down hard from high speed, and the walls lining the entire circuit mean safety cars are common - Montreal is one of the least predictable races on the calendar strategically because of how often the race gets interrupted.
