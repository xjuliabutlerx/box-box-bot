# Circuit Zandvoort

A narrow, undulating circuit along the Dutch coastline, built through sand dunes and notable for banked corners at Turn 3 and the final corner - a rarity in modern Formula 1 that lets cars carry unusually high minimum speed through what would otherwise be a slow hairpin. Zandvoort is tight and old-school in character despite being substantially reworked for its return to the calendar, with barriers close to the track for much of the lap.

Overtaking is difficult - the track is narrow and twisty with few long straights, so track position from qualifying carries significant weight, similar in spirit to Hungary or Monaco. Coastal weather can change quickly and bring rain with little warning, and sand blown onto the track from the surrounding dunes can affect grip, particularly off the racing line - a factor that makes overtaking attempts even riskier than the layout alone would suggest.
