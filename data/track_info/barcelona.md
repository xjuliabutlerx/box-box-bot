# Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya

A well-rounded permanent circuit that's also one of Formula 1's most common pre-season testing venues, meaning teams typically arrive with an unusually good understanding of how their car behaves here. It mixes fast, sweeping corners (particularly the demanding Turns 3 and 9) with a slower, more technical final sector, making it a genuine all-round test of a car's aerodynamic balance.

Historically Barcelona was known for being hard to overtake at outside of the two DRS zones on the pit straight and the back straight, though layout changes to the final sector in recent years have opened up the track and generally improved racing. High lateral loads through the fast corners make it one of the more demanding circuits on tires, and a two-stop strategy is common, particularly in hot conditions that accelerate degradation further.
