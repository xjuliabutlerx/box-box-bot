# Lusail International Circuit

A fast, flowing circuit originally built for motorcycle racing before being adapted for Formula 1, featuring a long sequence of high-speed corners that place enormous, sustained lateral load on tires - among the most physically demanding circuits on the calendar for exactly that reason. Races here are typically held at night under floodlights to avoid the desert heat.

Overtaking is moderate, aided by a long back straight and DRS zone, but the flowing middle sector offers little opportunity to pass. The circuit's high-speed corners historically produced significant tire wear, severe enough in early events to require mandated pit-stop minimums - a reminder that Lusail pushes tire limits harder than most circuits and that degradation management is a central strategic theme of the weekend, more so than at almost any other stop on the calendar.
