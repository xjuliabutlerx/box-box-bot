# Marina Bay Street Circuit

One of the most physically demanding races on the calendar: a bumpy, high-downforce street circuit through Singapore's Marina Bay district, traditionally raced at night under floodlights in extreme heat and humidity. The combination of a long race distance, high ambient temperature, and a track with more corners than almost anywhere else on the calendar makes it a serious endurance test for drivers.

Overtaking is difficult given the tight, wall-lined layout typical of a street circuit, so qualifying position matters heavily. Marina Bay has one of the highest safety-car rates on the calendar - the combination of a street track, tight margins, and a long race often produces at least one interruption, which makes strategy here unusually opportunistic: teams frequently hold back on pit stops in the hope of a safety car that lets them change tires at a fraction of the usual time cost.
