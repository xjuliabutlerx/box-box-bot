# Silverstone Circuit

The home of the British Grand Prix and one of the fastest, most demanding circuits on the calendar - the high-speed Maggotts-Becketts-Chapel complex is taken at enormous speed and generates some of the highest lateral loads a Formula 1 car experiences all season, making it a favorite among drivers for how much it rewards commitment and car balance.

Overtaking is possible but not easy - the Hangar Straight into Stowe and the start-finish straight into Turn 1 (Village/The Loop) are the main DRS zones, while the fast middle sector is mostly about maintaining momentum rather than passing outright. Tire wear through the high-speed corners is significant, and Britain's changeable weather adds a real chance of a wet or mixed-conditions race that throws strategy into chaos - Silverstone has produced some of the sport's most memorable strategic gambles for exactly that reason.
