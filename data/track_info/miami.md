# Miami International Autodrome

A 5.41 km circuit laid out in the parking lots and access roads surrounding Hard Rock Stadium, mixing tight, low-speed sections with a long, sweeping back straight and a fast, technical final sector. It plays more like a hybrid between a street circuit and a permanent track - wide enough for genuine racing, but still bounded by walls close enough to punish mistakes.

The back straight into Turn 11 is the main DRS overtaking zone, and the track generally produces good racing with multiple viable strategy windows. South Florida heat and humidity make tire and driver management a real factor, particularly in the tighter Turns 14-17 complex, and a safety car is a reasonably common feature of the race given the walls lining much of the lap - a real strategic variable teams have to plan around.
