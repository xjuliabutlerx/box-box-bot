# Hungaroring

A tight, twisty circuit often compared to a "go-kart track" for its lack of long straights and constant stream of medium-speed corners, set in a natural amphitheater outside Budapest. It's one of the most demanding circuits on tires and driver concentration precisely because there's nowhere to rest - the whole lap is a continuous sequence of direction changes.

Overtaking is notoriously difficult, on a par with Monaco despite being a permanent circuit rather than a street track, which makes qualifying position disproportionately important. The single DRS zone on the pit straight is often not enough on its own to complete a pass. Tire degradation is high given the constant lateral loading, so strategy variation between drivers is common even without external factors like a safety car forcing anyone's hand.
