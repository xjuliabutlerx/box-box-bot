# Las Vegas Strip Circuit

A purpose-built street circuit running down the middle of the Las Vegas Strip past several of the city's most famous casinos and landmarks, raced at night in cold desert-climate temperatures that contrast sharply with most late-season venues. The layout includes very long straights, giving the circuit some of the highest top speeds of the season despite being a street track.

The long straights make Las Vegas a strong overtaking circuit relative to most street layouts, with multiple DRS zones giving drivers repeated chances across a lap. The cold night-time temperatures are the defining strategic factor: they make it unusually hard to get tires into their working temperature range, particularly early in a stint, which affects both qualifying pace and in-race tire management in ways that don't apply at almost any other circuit on the calendar.
