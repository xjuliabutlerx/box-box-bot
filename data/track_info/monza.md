# Autodromo Nazionale Monza

Known as the "Temple of Speed," Monza is the fastest circuit on the calendar by average speed - long straights connected by a handful of heavy-braking chicanes, run with the lowest downforce setup of the season. Cars spend more of the lap at or near full throttle here than almost anywhere else, which flips the usual aerodynamic priorities: straight-line speed and braking stability matter more than cornering grip.

The low-downforce setup and multiple straights make Monza one of the best overtaking circuits on the calendar, particularly into the first and second chicanes. It's also Ferrari's home race, played in front of the Tifosi, which adds outsized pressure and media scrutiny regardless of the championship situation. Tire degradation is relatively low given how little cornering load the layout generates, and a one-stop strategy is typical, with slipstreaming in practice and qualifying a notable tactical element of the weekend.
